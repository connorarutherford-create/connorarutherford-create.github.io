About

I build tools that make sophisticated things feel simple.

Before that — before any of this — I was someone else entirely. A closed chapter I don't revisit.

Then I met someone who changed everything. I started over from zero. Not figuratively — emotionally, financially, directionally zero. Just a Mac, too much stubbornness, and the conviction that I could build a life on my own terms.

That became my pattern: build systems that solve real problems, make them elegant, then let them run. No investors. No co-founder. No audience. Just quiet, methodical work.

Today I live in my own stack — Cloudflare, GitHub Pages, macOS automation — and I ship tools that do one thing well. I don't chase trends. I don't optimize for attention. I build what's useful, keep it clean, and move on.

The throughline is simple: independence, earned piece by piece, on my own terms.
